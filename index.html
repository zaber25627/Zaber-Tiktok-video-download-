Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I download a TikTok video?

Copy the video link from TikTok, paste it into the box above, and press Analyze Video. When the details appear, choose one of the available options and press the download button.

What does "HD Without Watermark" mean?

It is the high-quality file that the lookup service returns for the video. It is offered only when the service actually provides it. If it is missing, you will see a message and nothing else is downloaded automatically. You can still choose another available option yourself.

Why is my video link not working?

The link must be a full TikTok video link. Private videos, deleted videos, region-restricted videos, or temporary problems with the third-party service can all cause the lookup to fail. Check the link and try again in a moment.

Why are some download options unavailable?

Only the options returned by the underlying service for that specific video are shown. If a version, such as audio or an alternative video file, is not returned, it cannot be offered.

Why are some creator statistics missing?

This site shows only the data the video lookup service returns. Creator details such as follower counts, following counts, total likes, and biography are not part of that response, so they are not displayed rather than guessed.

Why did my download fail?

The file is hosted by a third party and its link can expire or be blocked by your network or browser. Analyze the link again to get a fresh download link, or use "Open direct link" and save the file from the new tab.

Does the downloader work on mobile devices?

Yes. The site is built mobile-first and works in modern mobile browsers. Some mobile browsers open the file in a new tab instead of saving it directly. If that happens, use the browser's share or download option on the video.
